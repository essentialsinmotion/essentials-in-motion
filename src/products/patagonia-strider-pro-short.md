## Why this one

I’ve worn Strider Pros for roughly two hours a day over the past six years. They work for running, lifting, hiking, and general training, so I rarely need to think about which shorts to wear. I’m on my third pair. At that frequency, their cost per use has become negligible.

They’re light and close fitting without feeling restrictive. The waistband stays put, the seams are low profile, and there’s little extra fabric to bunch or flap. 

## In use

The thin recycled polyester shell dries quickly, and the integrated liner has remained comfortable through repeated use. They offer enough coverage for lifting while staying light enough for running. For a synthetic short I wear this often, they also handle odor better than I expected.

I sometimes wear them under pants on colder days or while traveling. Their low bulk makes that practical, which adds to their usefulness beyond running. The tradeoffs are straightforward: the liner won’t suit everyone, the light fabric isn’t built for heavy abrasion, and the pockets aren’t meant to carry much.

## What I’d change

Some storage could be expanded to take a phone better and the main failure point I’ve experienced is the seam elastic on the interior liner.
