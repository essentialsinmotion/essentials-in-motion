## Why this one

I used to wear thick leather belts and webbing belts that felt like they would last a lifetime. They held my pants up, but I was always aware of them when sitting, bending, or wearing a pack. After nearly a year of wearing the Atlas almost every day, I’ve come to appreciate the way it just disappears.

The stretch webbing moves with me without sagging. It holds heavier double-front pants as well as lighter travel trousers, and I rarely need to readjust it during the day. The slim polymer buckle also seems to put less wear on my merino t-shirts, leaving them run-free for longer.

## In use

Travel days made the difference especially clear. The belt stays comfortable through long stretches of sitting, then moves easily with me when I’m walking again. Its buckle has no metal (aka keep it on for security), and the belt is light enough that I don’t think about packing it.

It also works well with lightweight pants, where a thick leather belt can feel disproportionate. In black, it’s visually quiet enough for most of what I wear.

The stretch has a limit: I wouldn’t choose it to support heavy items clipped at my waist. That hasn’t affected my use, but it’s worth considering if you regularly carry gear on your belt.
