## Why this one

I’ve tried a lot of barefoot and minimalist footwear over the years. Shoes have come and gone, but these sandals have stayed. I use them for long trail runs, lifting, travel, and everyday wear. Having one familiar pair cover all of those activities is especially useful when I’m packing light.

The thin Vibram Megagrip sole gives me traction and ground feel without relying on foam that changes as it wears. Once I’ve adjusted the lacing, the sandals feel secure and largely disappear underfoot. I’ve put thousands of miles on a pair before wearing through the sole or reaching the end of the toe post attachment.

## In use

They’ve been comfortable on trail runs over 20 miles and stable for gym sessions. They pack flat for travel, and I can keep running or training away from home in the same footwear I use every day.

The footbed collects sweat, dust, and grit, so I try to remember to rinse it regularly. The heel strap can also slide down my Achilles and need readjustment. Neither issue has stopped me from using them, but both are noticeable.

These are thin, open sandals, and they take time to adapt to. I use them most in warm or temperate conditions. For cold weather or extended backcountry trips where exposed toes would be a problem, I opt for closed-toe shoes.

## What I’d change

I’d like the heel strap to stay in place more reliably on long runs, but am not willing to take on the weight of something like the Bedrock Cairn Evo Pro.
