## Why this one

I use the 1L Zippy to keep toiletries and small tech together inside a larger bag while traveling. It has enough depth for bulkier items without adding unnecessary bulk, organizational structure, or weight. 

I use more organizational aids when traveling in the front country. The combination of Pods and a Zippy adds structure without bulk or weight. The Pods hold clothing and things I don’t need during the day, while the Zippy keeps smaller essentials together in a kit I can easily grab or move between bags.

## In use

Inside the Zippy, I use smaller ditty bags to separate tech from toiletries. Placing sunglasses and eye mask along the sides helps give the pouch some shape, while 1-2 smaller bags organize the space between them. There’s no padding, so I pay attention to how fragile items sit against everything else.

1L works for me without coming near to over-stuffing it. When traveling, it works well as a standalone item. Later, after some consumables have disappeared, I can tuck it into a Pod and consolidate the load. That flexibility has made it useful across backpacks, duffels, and daily carry.

The fabric and zipper offer water resistance, but I don’t treat the pouch as waterproof. The fine zipper also works best when I leave enough room to close it easily rather than forcing in one more item.

## What I’d change

I hate waterproof zippers, especially when they are this small. End of story.
