## Why this one

I wanted a substantial sweatshirt I could wear often without treating it carefully. The Uprisal has filled that role for two years. I reach for it on cool mornings, work days, and evenings at home because it’s warm, comfortable, and easy to wear with the rest of my clothes.

The dense recycled cotton and polyester fleece gives it structure. It drapes well, holds its shape, and feels way more durable than lighter sweatshirts I’ve owned. The hood is proportionate and stays put, and the fit leaves enough room through the arms without feeling sloppy.

## In use

After more than 150 days of wear, the body fabric shows little pilling, the stitching is intact, and the hood still holds its shape. I initially wondered whether the thinner ribbed cuffs and hem would wear out first, but both have held up. The cuffs also stretch enough for me to push the sleeves over my forearms comfortably.

It’s best for dry, cool days and lower levels of activity. The cotton blend is heavy, takes up space in a bag, and dries slowly when wet. I use it as an everyday sweatshirt, not as a layer for active backcountry travel.

## What I’d change

I’d add a small hang loop. As it is, I usually hang the hoodie by its hood.
