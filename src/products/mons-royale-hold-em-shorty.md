## Why this one

I’ve worn merino underwear for nearly two decades, and the Hold ’Em Shorty has become my daily choice. It combines the odor resistance I want from wool with enough nylon and elastane to hold up to frequent wear and keep its shape.

I often wear a pair on consecutive days and wash it about once a week. After more than 300 days of use, the fabric, support, and waistband have remained consistent. That reliability lets me travel with just one pair confidently. 

## In use

The Shorty is comfortable under pants and shorts, stays in place, and feels supportive without squeezing. The shorter cut works particularly well for work, travel, and ordinary days. There’s no fly, which took me about a day to get used to. Instead, there are two layers of fabric over the groin, which I’ve found helpful when other fabric wears down over time. 

I did try wearing it on a high-mileage backcountry trip to save weight. The short inseam left my inner thighs exposed to friction, and I ended up using KT tape to manage chafing. For long hiking days, I use the longer Hold ’Em Boxer instead. The Shorty remains my choice for daily wear.

## What I’d change

Maybe adding a fly? Can’t decide.
