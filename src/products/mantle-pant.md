## Why this one

The Mantle became a daily driver soon after Black Diamond released it. The fit works so well for me that I bought multiple pairs: one in regular rotation and at least one backup. After burning through one pair, I now have a daily driver for fall/spring and a black pair I set aside for travel.

As far as fit goes, there’s room through the thighs and seat, with a gradual taper toward the ankle that keeps the legs tidy without restricting movement. They cover much of what I need from everyday pants. They don’t stain or snag easily and I can sit comfortably through a long travel day or wear them to work/ out to dinner. These are simple pants that hide their technicality well with subtle darts at the knee and a hidden 5th pocket tucked behind the side-seam mid-thigh. Paired with a t-shirt, hoodie, or button-down, the pants fit naturally into most of my daily routine.

## In use

After more than 300 days of wear, the stretch cotton–nylon fabric has held up well. It moves easily without feeling overly elastic, and the knees and seat retain their shape even after weeks of repeated wear between washes. The body fabric breathes well enough for most of the year, but I avoid these pants during the summer months.

I’ve seen minor fraying at a few stitch points, but the seams remain intact. The cotton blend dries more slowly than fully synthetic hiking pants, so I reach for something else when I expect sustained wet conditions.

Unfortunately, they’re discontinued, which is why I’m glad they last (and that I still have a backup pair).

## What I’d change

I’d remove the small zippered pocket on the right thigh. I don’t need it, and I’d prefer the cleaner design without it. Mostly, though, I’d like Black Diamond to make these again.
