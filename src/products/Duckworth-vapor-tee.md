## Why this one

Lightweight merino tees are comfortable, but I’ve often found them too fragile for regular wear. The Vapor’s merino and nylon blend keeps the softness and odor resistance I want while holding up better in daily rotation. It’s become an easy choice for travel, everyday wear, and light activity.

The fabric has more structure than many thin merino shirts without feeling stiff or itchy. I also like the relaxed cut and minimal branding.

## In use

I can wear it multiple times between washes without odor becoming a problem. At approximately 140 g/m², the fabric breathes well but doesn’t feel delicate, and it has held its shape through repeated wear. The heathered color ways hide stains while on the road! 

It does pill in high-friction areas. After nearly five years, I’ve also seen small tears begin to develop near the beltline, where the shirt repeatedly meets a waistband, belts, or zipper. That’s a longer useful life than I’ve gotten from many lightweight merino tees, even if it doesn’t stay looking new.

## What I’d change

I’d like a few solid color options. The heathered fabric hides wear well, but I don’t always want that look.
