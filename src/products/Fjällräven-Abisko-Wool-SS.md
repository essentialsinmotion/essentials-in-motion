## Why this one

I’ve spent years looking for a wool T-shirt that fits well, looks ordinary, and survives frequent wear. The Abisko Wool SS comes closer than most. I wear these shirts daily. 

The merino and polyester blend gives me much of the comfort and odor resistance I like about wool with better durability than the pure merino tees I’ve owned. The fit is athletic without clinging; a large works well for me even though I often fall between medium and large. The small embroidered chest logo is easy to overlook.

## In use

After two years in regular rotation, the collars have kept their shape. I still have my first shirt, though small holes eventually appeared in high-friction areas. It now serves as a sleep shirt. That feels like a reasonable lifespan for something I’ve worn this often (hundreds of wears and dozens of washes).

The odor resistance makes a difference when I travel. I usually rotate between one or two shirts, turning the worn one inside out and hanging it overnight. That has worked well on trips lasting two to four weeks. The fabric loses a little of its initial softness over time, but it remains comfortable.
