*Edited October 2026*

Travel is just a little different than everyday life. It falls outside the norm: new places, new experiences, new everything. Having a recognizable and reliable system for travel can create dependability and consistency as you navigate all the other variables in flux. 

*This is not all inclusive, but is demonstrative of core items that make the cut regardless of duration, location, etc.*

### Worn

#### Fjallraven Abisko T-shirt

Surprise surprise.

#### Black Diamond Mantle Pant

#### Mons Royale Hold 'em Shorty Underwear

May shift between the Shorty or the regular length depending on season or activity level. 

#### Darn Tough Micro Crew Mid Cushion Sock

Ideal pairing with boots. May adjust to the 1/4 height if wearing lower shoes.

#### Brimmed Hat

#### Ombraz Sunglasses

Arm-less shades take up less room and are less likely to break.

#### Luna Leadville Pacer / Blundstone Classics

Footwear will ultimately depend on conditions, but I prefer the Pacer’s whenever possible because running/working out in Blundstones is comical.

#### Sambob Alpha 120 Pullover Hoodie

#### Black Diamond 3L Deploy Shell
